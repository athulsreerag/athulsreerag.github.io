III · The letter

A small confession

To my dearest Shaalu,

I have to begin by thanking your sister — quietly, sincerely — for the small part she played in making this story possible. You don't need to know the details, but I do.

There is an image of you, somewhere, wearing a Kerala saree, the watch on your wrist catching the light. I saved it. I look at it more than I should admit. It is, somehow, the most honest photograph of a person I have ever seen.

I didn't want to write something generic. I didn't want a message that could be sent to anyone. I wanted a real, specific, slightly imperfect thing — the kind of thing you keep.

So here it is. Five chapters, five photographs, one question. Take your time.

— yours, always
